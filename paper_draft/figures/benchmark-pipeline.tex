\caption{\textbf{SecDevQA construction pipeline.} Three phases
(\S\ref{sec:phase-sourcing}--\S\ref{sec:phase-normalization}). Phase~1 sources
candidate threads from \dnum{10} repositories; Phase~2 extracts candidate Q\&A
pairs with a two-stage LLM pipeline and screens them down to \dnum{158}
human-verified pairs (\dnum{105} stating an externally checkable hard fact);
Phase~3 normalizes \dnum{56} pairs into self-contained, resolution-free
evaluation items (\dnum{44} contextual, \dnum{12} parametric) under independent
dual annotation, and codes the corpus (open then axial) toward a question
taxonomy. Blue steps are automated (LLM); orange steps are human-in-the-loop;
green cylinders are released artifacts. Counts reflect the current state of
construction and grow as the protocol is applied to more repositories.}
